## INSTRUCTIONS

Evaluate the post as a whole. In Vietnamese social media, sarcasm often emerges from the *interaction* between text and image — but only when there is a genuine semantic gap, not just thematic overlap.

### Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Process:

1. **Standalone Signals Summary**: Briefly restate what the text says literally and what the image shows visually. Do not re-analyze — summarize.

2. **Cross-Modal Relationship**: Identify which of these patterns applies:
   - **Reinforcing**: Text and image convey the same message sincerely → lean toward 0
   - **Contradicting**: Text says X, image shows clearly not-X → strong signal for 1
   - **Ironic framing**: Text provides sarcastic commentary on an otherwise neutral image → 1
   - **Meme amplification**: Image is a known sarcastic meme template + text aligns with it → 1
   State which pattern applies and explain why.

3. **Contradiction Verification**: If you identified "Contradicting" or "Ironic framing" above — describe the exact contradiction. What specifically does the text claim? What specifically does the image show that contradicts it? If you cannot state a concrete contradiction, default to the Reinforcing pattern.

4. **Final Verdict**: Label 1 only if step 3 confirms a concrete, verifiable contradiction or ironic framing. A general feeling of "something is off" is not sufficient.
