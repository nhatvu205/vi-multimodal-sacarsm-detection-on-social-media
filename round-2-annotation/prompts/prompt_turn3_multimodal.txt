Your task is to determine whether a given post (TEXT + IMAGE) is non-sarcastic (label 0) or sarcastic (label 1) by analyzing them TOGETHER as a single, unified message.

## INSTRUCTIONS

Sarcasm in Vietnamese social media does NOT require a contradiction between text and image.
It can emerge from either modality alone, from both simultaneously, or from their combination creating a new meaning.

### Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Process:

1. **Modality Summary**:
   - Text signal: Summarize what the text says literally. State its standalone sarcasm signal (T=1) or lack thereof (T=0).
   - Image signal: Summarize what the image shows. State its standalone sarcasm signal (I=1) or lack thereof (I=0).

2. **Cross-Modal Pattern Identification**:
   Identify which of the following 7 patterns best describes this post:

   *Sarcastic patterns:*
   - [S-A] I=1, T=0 — Image is the primary sarcasm carrier; text is neutral support
   - [S-B] I=0, T=1 — Text is the primary sarcasm carrier; image is neutral support
   - [S-C] I=1, T=1 — Both modalities are independently sarcastic; combination amplifies
   - [S-D] I=0, T=0 → combined=1 — Neither is sarcastic alone, but their combination creates irony or mockery

   *Non-sarcastic patterns:*
   - [N-A] I=0, T=0 — Both are sincere; no irony emerges from combination
   - [N-B] I=1, T=0 → combined=0 — Image has mild irony but text neutralizes or redirects the meaning
   - [N-C] I=0, T=1 → combined=0 — Text has mild irony but image neutralizes or redirects the meaning

   State the pattern code (e.g., [S-B]) and explain in 1–2 sentences why it applies.

3. **Evidence Verification**:
   - For S-A or S-C: What specific visual element or OCR text in the image carries the sarcasm?
   - For S-B or S-C: What specific phrase or linguistic signal in the text carries the sarcasm?
   - For S-D: What new ironic meaning emerges *only* when text and image are read together? Describe the emergent meaning concretely.
   - For N-B or N-C: Why does the stronger modality fail to make the whole post sarcastic? What does the other modality do to cancel or redirect it?
   - For N-A: Confirm both modalities are sincere.

4. **Final Verdict**: Assign llm_label=1 for any S-pattern, llm_label=0 for any N-pattern. The conclusion must reference the pattern code and the specific evidence from step 3.
